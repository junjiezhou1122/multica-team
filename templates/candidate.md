# Memory candidate

- Proposed entry: a single knowledge bullet with source, added date, scope and uncertainty.
- Destination: member, project or team path.
- Existing related knowledge: root-relative links.
- Evidence: source references and what they establish.
- Limitations: what remains unverified.
- Origin: attributed member or source without private identifiers in a public instance.
- Decision: pending, accepted with destination, or declined with reason.
